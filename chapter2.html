Modern teachers
The roots of modernism go back to the 1920s. As an artistic and philosophical direction, modernism significantly shaped European society, which was changing at the time as a result of rapid modernization combined with the development of industry, technology, population expansion and urban growth.
Modernism called for the end of the old social orders and the establishment of a new beginning of the history. The architecture of the new age was supposed to fulfill the requirements of the
new man
. Its form was no longer to follow tradition, but function.
