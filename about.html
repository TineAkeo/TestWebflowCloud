Dare mighty things.

Impossible is not a declaration, it is a dare. We have limited time as a society to avert irreversible climate change and we need to take as many winning shots as we can. We build high-performance teams who enjoy playing together and are innately driven to take daring but calculated risks to win. We will find a way.

Simplify to scale.

We need to achieve significant scale quickly to accomplish our mission and we believe simpler systems scale faster and more efficiently. We believe deletion is the highest form of simplification. We lean on first principles to decouple and break dependencies and only then invent new solutions.
