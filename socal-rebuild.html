Rebuilding after a wildfire shouldn’t take years. Reframe delivers fire-resilient homes faster and at attainable prices—with one partner from permits to move-in.

Rebuilding shouldn’t be this hard
Wildfires have left thousands of California families without safe housing. Traditional construction takes years, with rising costs and endless uncertainty. Families deserve homes they can count on — faster, safer, and without the stress of juggling multiple contractors.

One partner
One timeline
No surprises

Reframe is vertically integrated, meaning we handle everything for you. From permits and design to construction and delivery, you get one accountable partner, clear timelines, and a home designed to stand up to today’s climate.
